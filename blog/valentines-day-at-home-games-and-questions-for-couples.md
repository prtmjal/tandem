A good Valentine's Day at home needs three parts: a warm-up, some play and a quiet close. This plan gives you 16 questions and six games to fill them, costs almost nothing, and works whether you are on the same sofa or on a video call. Valentine's Day 2027 falls on Sunday 14 February, so there is time to plan.

If Valentine's Day is not your thing this year, whether you are single, grieving or simply tired of the fuss, that is completely fine. These ideas work just as well on any ordinary evening, and for friends as much as partners.

## Why stay in?

Restaurants on the 14th tend to mean fixed menus, tight tables and a rushed feel. At home you choose the pace, the food and the music, and you can stop any time you like. Because the date lands on a Sunday in 2027, you could also pick Friday or Saturday evening and keep Sunday for something easy.

Here is the shape of the evening.

## Part 1: The warm-up (about 20 minutes)

Make the room feel a little different. Dim the lights, light a candle if you like, and put the kettle on. Hot chocolate, tea and a favourite snack are all you need. Then ask each other a few easy questions to get talking.

### Five warm-up questions

1. What is the best small thing that happened to you this week?
2. What is a meal you would happily eat every month for the rest of your life?
3. Which of our shared jokes still makes you smile?
4. If we had a free afternoon tomorrow, how would you spend it?
5. What is a tiny thing I do that you would miss if I stopped?

## Part 2: The play (about 60 minutes)

Now the games. Choose three or four, not all six. Each one is meant to be quick.

### Six games for two

1. **The love-note swap.** Each of you has three minutes to write a short note on paper to your partner, then you swap and read them aloud. Keep them warm and specific.
2. **Two truths and a hope.** Say two true things about your week and one hope for the year. Your partner guesses which is the hope.
3. **Rank the date.** Each of you privately lists your top three Valentine's evenings ever, real or imagined, then you compare. Matches earn a cheer.
4. **Cook-off for two.** Make a simple dessert together, each in charge of half the steps. Judge the results with fake scorecards.
5. **Memory lane.** Take turns describing a photo from your phones without showing it. Your partner guesses where and when it was taken.
6. **Plan the dream weekend.** Each of you writes a three-line plan for a perfect weekend away, then you read both aloud and steal the best bits.

For something more structured, [who's more likely to questions for couples](/blog/whos-more-likely-to-questions-for-couples) are made for evenings like this, and the [couples quiz](/blog/couples-quiz-how-well-do-you-know-each-other) is another easy one.

### Eleven more questions, if the conversation flows

6. What is a moment from this past year you would like to keep forever?
7. What makes you feel most appreciated by me?
8. What is a compliment you remember, and why did it stay with you?
9. Where did you feel the happiest with me, and what made it so?
10. What is something you would like us to do more often?
11. What song would be the soundtrack for tonight?
12. What is one thing you admire about how I handle hard days?
13. What would you like our next Valentine's Day to look like?
14. What is the nicest thing someone has done for you without being asked?
15. What is a small adventure we could have before spring?
16. Complete this: "I feel closest to you when..."

## Part 3: The close (about 10 minutes)

End gently. Say thank you for the evening, and share one thing you are looking forward to in the next week. Then put something small on the calendar, such as a walk on Saturday or a movie night next month. Ending with a plan makes the night feel like the start of something, not a one-off.

If you want a calm last question, ask each other: "What was your favourite moment tonight?"

## If you are apart

Distance does not rule out any of this. Light a candle on each end, make the same hot drink, and keep the video call open. The love-note swap, two truths and a hope, memory lane and the dream weekend all work by camera. For more ideas, see [FaceTime games for couples](/blog/facetime-games-for-couples) and [long-distance relationship games](/blog/long-distance-relationship-games). You could also send each other one question at a time and take turns answering.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is Valentine's Day in 2027?**
Sunday 14 February 2027. Many couples choose the Friday or Saturday before for their evening in.

**How much does a Valentine's evening at home cost?**
It can cost close to nothing. A hot drink, a snack you love and paper for the notes are enough.

**How do we do this on a video call?**
Keep the call open, match your drinks and take turns with the games. The questions work just as well spoken aloud over a screen.

**What if one of us does not enjoy Valentine's Day?**
Then call it a cosy night in and skip the theme. The warm-up, play and close still work.
