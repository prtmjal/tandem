## A heading for each main point

Write at least 150 words in total. Plain paragraphs, **bold**, lists and [links](/play) all work.

- Name the file in lowercase words joined by hyphens, for example `date-night-questions.md`. That becomes the address: /blog/date-night-questions
- Remove the `draft: true` line when the post is ready to publish.
- Pictures go in `public/img/blog/` and are linked like this: `![What the picture shows](/img/blog/name.jpg)`
- Only quote cards from Levels 1 to 3, and keep it suggestive at most, like the game.
