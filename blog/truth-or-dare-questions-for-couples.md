Here are 40 truth questions and 25 dares for couples, written to be flirty and playful without ever getting explicit. One house rule makes the whole game work: everyone gets a free pass, with no explanation needed.

## The house rule that makes it work

Agree on three rules before you start:

1. **One free pass per person, per round.** Say "pass" and move on. You never have to explain, and the other person never asks why.
2. **Nobody is ever forced.** If a truth feels too big tonight or a dare feels off, skipping is a normal move, not a loss.
3. **A dare is something you could do in front of a stranger.** If you would feel awkward doing it in a café, it is not a dare for this game.

That third rule keeps everything light: silly voices, small romantic gestures and a bit of showing off.

To play, take turns. Whoever is up picks "truth" or "dare", and the other reads one from the lists below. Stop while it is still fun.

## 40 truths

### Funny

1. What is the silliest thing you have ever been sure you were right about?
2. Which of your own habits would you least like a film crew to follow around?
3. What is the most ridiculous thing you have ever bought?
4. If you had to enter a talent show tomorrow, what would you perform?
5. What is a song you know every word to but would never admit to in public?
6. What is the worst haircut or outfit you have ever loved?
7. What would your animal sidekick be, and what would you name it?
8. What is a small lie you have told so often it is now part of your story?
9. Which of us would last longer in a dance-off, and why is it you?
10. What is the funniest thing you have ever done while thinking nobody was watching?

### Sweet

11. What is a tiny thing I do that always makes you smile?
12. When did you last feel really proud of me?
13. What is a moment from our first month together that you still think about?
14. What is something I say that you would miss if I stopped?
15. When do you feel most looked after by me?
16. What is the best compliment I have ever given you?
17. What is one thing you have always wanted to thank me for?
18. Which ordinary evening with me would you happily live again?
19. What did you notice about me first?
20. What makes you feel closest to me on a busy day?

### Getting to know you

21. What was your favourite place to hide or daydream as a child?
22. What is something you changed your mind about in the last few years?
23. What is a small thing you are quietly working on in yourself?
24. Which teacher, friend or relative shaped you the most?
25. What is a meal that instantly makes you feel at home?
26. What do you wish people asked you about more often?
27. What is a skill you would love to pick up this year?
28. When do you do your best thinking?
29. What is something you are braver about now than you used to be?
30. What does a perfect lazy Sunday look like for you?

### Daydreaming

31. If we could swap lives for a week, what would you want to try first?
32. Where would you take me if we had a free weekend and no limits?
33. What would our dream house have that no other house does?
34. If we opened a tiny shop together, what would it sell?
35. Which decade would you visit for one day, and would you bring me?
36. What would a perfect surprise from me look like?
37. If we wrote a book together, what would it be about?
38. Where do you hope we are celebrating in five years?
39. What is one thing you would love us to do just once, even if it is a bit silly?
40. If tonight could end any way you like, what is the nicest, simplest version?

## 25 dares

### Silly

1. Give your partner a heartfelt compliment in a funny voice. Pick the voice yourself.
2. Do your best impression of your partner, kindly.
3. Narrate the next minute of what your partner is doing like a nature documentary.
4. Say "I love you" three different ways without repeating a word.
5. Make up a short theme tune for your partner and perform it.
6. Do a one-minute dance, with full commitment.
7. Invent a handshake for the two of you.

### Romantic

8. Hold your partner's gaze for thirty seconds without laughing. Laughing is allowed, honestly.
9. Slow dance to one song, wherever you are standing.
10. Draw a hand-drawn portrait of your partner in two minutes, then sign it.
11. Write a short handwritten note and hide it somewhere your partner will find it tomorrow.
12. Read out a text your partner sent you that you liked, and say why.
13. Tell the story of how you met as if it were a film trailer.
14. Plan a date in one minute and pitch it like a sales presentation.
15. Pick a song for your partner and play it right now.
16. Give your partner a three-sentence love letter, read aloud with feeling.

### Do it right now

17. Invent a secret hand squeeze that means "I am glad you are here", then teach it.
18. Text your partner one thing you love about them, even though they are in the room.
19. Make your partner a small cup of something warm, no questions asked.
20. Put a song on and each of you plays air guitar for fifteen seconds.
21. Tidy one small thing your partner has been meaning to get to.
22. Pick one photo of the two of you and tell the story behind it.
23. Name three things you want to do together this month and add one to the calendar.
24. Say thank you for three specific things from this week.

## Variations

- **The timer round.** Set a timer for five minutes. Take as many turns as you can before it ends, with a pass each.
- **The swap-roles round.** Instead of choosing for yourself, your partner chooses "truth" or "dare" for you. Choose kindly, with a guess about what they would enjoy.
- **The long-distance version.** On a video call, keep the dares to things you can do on camera: the silly-voice compliment, the dance, the portrait held up to the screen. Our [video call game ideas](/blog/facetime-games-for-couples) have more to pair with it.

## Make it your own rules

The best version is the one you shape together. Agree on a few details first:

- **How many passes?** One per round is a good start. Some couples prefer two.
- **What is off the table tonight?** Say it once at the start.
- **How does it end?** Pick a stopping point, such as ten turns or one song.

Then write a few prompts of your own from a real moment, like a trip or a running joke.

## Frequently asked questions

**What makes truth or dare okay for couples who like to keep it light?**
Agree on the rules first: one free pass each, nobody forced, and dares you could do in front of a stranger.

**How many truths and dares should we do in one go?**
Ten to fifteen turns is plenty. Stopping while you are still laughing makes you more likely to play again.

**What if a truth is too personal?**
Pass. A pass needs no reason and no apology, and the next prompt is one tap away.

**Can we play truth or dare on a video call?**
Yes. Read the truths aloud and choose dares that work on camera, like a dance or a quick drawing.
