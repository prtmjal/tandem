The best FaceTime games for couples are simple ones: you need a camera, a few minutes and one rule you both understand. Below are 16 you can start right now without downloading anything, plus a plain walkthrough of the free two-phone mode in Nod Twice built for couples who are apart.

A quick note on search terms: people look for "games to play with your boyfriend or girlfriend on FaceTime", and everything here works for any couple. We just say "your partner".

## Question games

1. **Three-minute rapid fire.** Set a timer. Take turns asking quick either/or questions: mountains or beach, early or late, call or text. No explaining until the timer ends.
2. **One good thing, one odd thing.** Each of you shares the best moment of your day and the strangest one. It turns "how was your day?" into a story.
3. **Ask, then flip it.** One person asks a question such as "What are you looking forward to this month?" The other answers, then asks the same thing back.
4. **Desert island list.** Each of you names three things you would bring and defends them. Then swap and guess which one your partner would cut first.
5. **Future postcard.** Describe a day one year from now, in detail, as if you are writing a postcard. Compare where your postcards overlap.

If you want a bigger stock of prompts, [what to talk about when you run out of things to say](/blog/what-to-talk-about-when-you-run-out-of-things-to-say) has plenty that suit a call.

## Guessing games

6. **Guess my answer.** Write a question on paper, such as "my favourite meal", and answer it secretly. Your partner guesses. Swap. Keep a score only if you both enjoy that.
7. **Who's more likely to.** Pick a silly situation ("who is more likely to get lost on holiday?"). Count to three and point at the screen or at yourself. Our list of [who's more likely to questions](/blog/whos-more-likely-to-questions-for-couples) gives you plenty to start with.
8. **The newlywed-style quiz.** Ask each other what your partner's pet peeve is, or what they would order at your favourite restaurant. [Newlywed game questions](/blog/newlywed-game-questions-for-couples) work for any couple, married or not.
9. **Two truths and a story.** Tell three things about your week, two true and one invented. Your partner guesses which one is made up.
10. **Draw it blind.** One person draws an object without looking at the page while the other watches and guesses.

## Games with a screen and a little movement

11. **Show and tell.** Pick a category ("something on my desk", "something older than ten years") and run to find one. Whoever is back first goes first.
12. **Room tour with a twist.** Give your partner a tour of one room, but they have to guess where something is hidden before you reach it.
13. **Charades by video.** Act out a film, a song or a shared memory. Your partner guesses. It works best with a theme you both know.
14. **Same recipe, same time.** Choose a simple snack, make it together on camera and rate each other's results.
15. **Parallel watch.** Press play on the same short video at the same moment, then react live.
16. **Photo roulette.** Each pick one photo from your phone from the last year and tell the story behind it before showing it.

## Making a video call feel less like a call

- **Pick an end time.** "Twenty minutes, then I'll be off to bed" removes the pressure to fill the evening.
- **Prop the phone up.** Hands free feels more like sitting together than holding a window.
- **Let it be quiet.** A pause while one of you thinks is not a failure.
- **Mix a game with a chat.** Play two rounds, then just talk about whatever came up.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do these games need any special app?**

No. The first fifteen need only a video call and perhaps a pen and paper. Nod Twice runs in a normal phone browser.

**How long should a FaceTime game last?**

Twenty to thirty minutes is plenty. A short call you both enjoy beats a long one where you are watching the clock.

**Can we play if one of us is on Android?**

Yes, for anything that works over any video app. Nod Twice runs in a browser on both kinds of phone.

**What if my partner is shy about playing?**

Start with the lightest games, like rapid fire, and let your partner pass anything. When passing is easy, people relax.
