Most couples games are a box of cards on a table, or an app you both have to download. Tandem After Dark is neither. One of you starts a game, sends a link, and you each play on your own phone, in the same room or on a video call.

## Why two phones?

Because some answers are easier to give privately. Before a night starts, each of you picks how far tonight can go, on your own screen. The game plays at the lower of the two picks, and neither of you sees what the other chose. Nobody has to be the one who says "not that far tonight" out loud.

The same idea runs through the whole game. Later levels open only when you have both said you are curious. Hands-on cards ask each of you first, privately, and quietly swap themselves out if either of you would rather not.

## What a night looks like

A night is 12 or 16 cards. It starts light and builds toward the level you chose, then slows down again before the end.

Early cards are for talking. One of them: "What small thing reliably improves your day? Tell me, then ask me the same." Another: "Name one thing I do that makes you feel supported. Be specific."

Not every card is a question. Some ask you to guess each other's answer, some ask "who's more likely to", and once a night you each rate a short list of ideas. Only the ones you are both open to appear, and they are saved for next time.

You can pass any card with one tap, and Pass looks exactly like Next. Either of you can pause or end the night at any point.

## How it ends

The last card is always a thank-you of some kind. Then you both tap to open an envelope with one small idea for the days ahead.

## What you need

Two phones and a browser. There is no download and no account, and your progress stays on your own phones. You both need to be 18 or older.
