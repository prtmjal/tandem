## 3. Content formats that win and typical lengths

### Takeaway
Long numbered/categorised lists dominate; counts range 20-300+. Winners tend to pair a list with categories, a how-to-use intro and FAQ. Game-style pages are mostly lists, not tools, except app-blog "generators".

### Cited Findings
- Observed list sizes: would you rather 36 (Bridebook UK) to 300+ (MomJunction); Marriage.com 100+; boyfriend questions 125-325; truth-or-dare 115-300 (Marriage.com, Wargamer); who's most likely 75-184; check-in 20-50; anniversary 20-73; LDR games 12-37; at-home dates 15-52. — sources in Section 2
- Candle organises 150 would-you-rather questions by mood and carries a 2026 date; Bridebook, Candle, Connected Couples show "(2026)"/updated stamps. — [Candle](https://www.trycandle.app/blog/would-you-rather-questions-for-couples)
- Generator/tool pages exist for truth or dare (teambuilding.com, codeitbro, truthordareai, many) and for couples (ourcouple.app, lovebae.app). — [serp_competition notes](https://www.codeitbro.com/tool/truth-or-dare-couples)
- Printable cards are a common supporting format (DateYourSpouse, Winshape PDF). — [DateYourSpouse](https://dateyourspouse.com/?p=57116)
- Paired's growth came from content scale plus a tool (Clearscope case study: 2,000% organic lift in under a year). — [Clearscope](https://clearscope.io/customers/paired)

### Inferences
- A new site cannot win on count (300 vs 300). Win on: a short, curated list (15-30) that is genuinely good, each section ending in "play this one with both answers hidden" linking to a room, plus FAQ and a printable/shareable card.
- Landing-page rule (tests/landing.test.ts, CLAUDE.md): posts may only quote Level 1-3 card text, so every list should be drawn from real Level 1-3 cards. This caps the "spicy" angle by design.
- Dating-year stamps ("2026") and UK/AU wording are cheap freshness signals.

### Gaps
- Word counts by SERP not measured; "typical length" is inferred from list counts.

## 6. Risky topics for an adult-adjacent site

### Takeaway
Keep every page at Level 1-3 wording, avoid "sex"/"dirty"/"kinky"/"fetish"/toys and sexual-health or sexual-wellness framing; "spicy" is a mild risk. Google's own policies restrict sexual content and dating ads, and SafeSearch can suppress sexual-wellness sites.

### Cited Findings
- Google Ads Sexual content policy (updated Oct 14, 2026, clarifying examples, no stated enforcement change); sexual health & wellness policy limits ads to select products/locations and no pleasure framing. — [Google Sexual content](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6023699?hl=en); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-spells-out-what-counts-as-sexually-suggestive-in-google-ads/); [Sexual health & wellness](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15275640?hl=en)
- Dating and companionship ads need certification (March 2025) and may be restricted by SafeSearch setting and sexual queries. — [Google](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15327789?hl=en-AU)
- Lovehoney reported its organic results and ads were blocked by SafeSearch even for advice content. — [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2022/07/20/lovehoney-produces-guide-turning-google-safesearch-access-site)
- Google removes pages that violate content policies regardless of SafeSearch setting; explicit-content guidelines exist. — [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/explicit/guidelines)
- Many competitor lists mix sexual/"mature" questions among date lists (noted by the anniversary-search summary); not an advisable model. — [Marriage.com anniversary](https://www.marriage.com/advice/love/anniversary-questions/)

### Inferences
- Avoid as targets: "sex questions for couples", "dirty truth or dare", "kinky/fantasy/bedroom questions", "intimacy questions" (ambiguous), "sex games", anything about toys/lubricant/sexual wellness, and "spicy" in H1/URL for the first batch. Use "flirty", "romantic", "playful", "closer".
- Mention the shop/Toy-box nowhere on blog posts (rule 11).
- No evidence found of a traffic penalty for mild couples content; SafeSearch risk rises with sexual vocabulary on the page, so keep copy and titles clean. This is judgment, not tested.

### Gaps
- No direct test of SafeSearch classification of nodtwice.com pages; recommend checking with SafeSearch on after launch.
