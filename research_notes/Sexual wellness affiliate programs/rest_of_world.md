# Sexual wellness affiliate options and legal restrictions: rest of world (outside India, US/Canada, UK/EU), as of Nov 2026

Method note: research was done with web search on 2026-11-05. Full-page fetches of some sources failed (g2risksolutions.com and mysteryvibe.com are blocked in this session's network settings), so many findings rest on search-result snippets and on affiliate directory aggregators (FlexOffers, affi.io, Shopper.com, Affilitizer, LinkClicky). Those aggregators are often stale and contradict each other. Treat every commission and cookie figure here as "verify in the network dashboard after joining". Nothing here is legal advice. Every "show no links" country needs local legal advice before the policy is relaxed.

## 1. Australia / New Zealand: which retailer, which network, what terms

### Takeaway
Lovehoney AU on Commission Factory is the clearest, best-documented option for Australia (about 22% commission; cookie stated as 30 days on the current listing, 90 days in older material). Wild Secrets (AU and NZ) and Adulttoymegastore AU are plausible secondary options but their network status is unconfirmed. Amazon AU's rate and adult-content policy for this category could not be confirmed. "Peachy Keen" in search results is a fashion boutique, not a sexual wellness retailer.

## 2. Brazil and Latin America (Mexico, Argentina, Colombia)

### Takeaway
Sexual wellness retail is legal and growing across Brazil, Mexico, Argentina and Colombia, but documented affiliate options for this category are thin. The only concrete named option found is Dona Coelha (Brazilian couples/female-pleasure sex shop) on Lomadee. Marketplace programs (Mercado Livre, Shopee BR, Amazon BR/MX) exist, but their rules on promoting adult-category items as an affiliate were not found, and Shopee's affiliate rules elsewhere explicitly exclude sex toys.

### Cited Findings
- Dona Coelha, an online sex shop "specialised in female and couples' pleasure", is listed as a brand on Lomadee (commission rate not shown) — [Lomadee: Dona Coelha](https://www.lomadee.com.br/en/brands/dona-coelha)
- Lomadee uses a CPA model with R$100 minimum withdrawal; a blog claims 5–15% commissions (not an official table) — [iDinheiro Lomadee guide](https://www.idinheiro.com.br/ganhar-dinheiro/afiliados/como-ganhar-dinheiro-afiliado-lomadee/)
- Awin operates in Brazil; a 2026 guide gives 3–15% general Awin BR commissions, not specific to adult — [Divulga Ninja 2026 guide](https://www.divulganinja.com.br/blog/melhores-programas-afiliados-brasil-2026-guia-completo)
- Shopee launched a Brazil affiliate program with commissions up to 14% (category rules for adult items not stated) — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/477797/exclusivo-shopee-lanca-programa-de-afiliados-no-brasil-com-comissoes-de-ate-14/)
- Mercado Livre allows adult products to be sold in an "Adultos" category restricted to 18+ with specific selling rules (seller-side, not affiliate-side) — [Nubimetrics BR](https://academia.nubimetrics.com/br/produtos-eroticos)

- Mexico / Spanish-speaking LatAm: Platanomelón is a natural candidate (couples-friendly brand, regional shipping) but no affiliate program was confirmed; contact directly. Amazon MX is a fallback for neutral items.

## 3. Southeast and East Asia: legality and affiliate options

### Takeaway
Legality splits sharply. Japan and South Korea: sale is legal or not prohibited (Korea has no specific rule on adult toys; customs historically blocked sex dolls but loosened in 2022). Thailand and Malaysia: sale is illegal under obscenity law with active enforcement. Indonesia and Vietnam: hostile or ambiguous with seizures. Singapore and Philippines: legal status not confirmed by this research. The big regional affiliate programs are unusable for toys: Shopee's affiliate rules prohibit promoting sex toys and pay no commission on them; Lazada PH bars sexual content and sexual-ability claims in affiliate content.

### Cited Findings
- Shopee affiliate social media guidelines list posts promoting sexual wellness items, including sex toys, as prohibited content; breaches can mean temporary ban or termination — [Shopee MY affiliate social media guidelines](https://help.shopee.com.my/portal/10/article/140075-[ENG]-Shopee-Affiliates-Social-Media-Community-Guidelines)
- Shopee: purchases of prohibited items are "NOT eligible for affiliate commission" — [Shopee SG FAQs on prohibited items](https://help.shopee.sg/portal/10/article/132082-FAQs%20on%20Prohibited%20Items); [Shopee MY FAQ](https://help.shopee.com.my/10/article/159320-%5BENG%5D-FAQs-on-Prohibited-Items)
- Shopee Live: from 26 March 2025 affiliates may not stream sex toys or performance enhancement products — [Shopee MY Live guidelines](https://help.shopee.com.my/portal/10/article/130726-[ENG]-Shopee-Live-Community-Guidelines)
- Shopee seller policy prohibits sex toys "used for or to facilitate sexual acts or pleasure but excluding contraceptive devices" — [Shopee SG prohibited items policy](https://help.shopee.sg/portal/article/77151)
- Lazada PH: sex toys/dolls excluded from Sponsored Solutions; sexually explicit or suggestive product content not allowed; affiliate terms prohibit sexual/pornographic promotional content and claims that a product enhances sexual ability — [Lazada PH product eligibility](https://community.lazada.com.ph/article/product-eligibility-1515700-hc); [Lazada PH affiliate terms](https://www.lazada.com.ph/wow/i/ph/corp/affiliate-terms)
- Lazada SG: sellers may not sell items restricted or prohibited by law in their country; SG affiliate program run by Lazada Singapore Pte Ltd — [Lazada SG prohibited and controlled products](https://community.lazada.sg/article/overview-of-prohibited-and-controlled-products-policy-455900-hc)
- Thailand: sex toys treated as obscene objects under Criminal Code Section 287 (up to 3 years' prison); import barred under Customs Act B.E. 2560; customs confiscated 4,000+ toys in one year — [The Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/news/national/sex-toys-popular-in-thailand-despite-conservative-laws); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/thailand/40026922)
- Thailand reform: Move Forward bill to amend Section 287 (2024) failed first reading in August 2024; Democrat Party backed decriminalisation. No 2025–2026 change found — [Bangkok Post](https://bangkokpost.com/thailand/politics/2833146/adult-biz-reform-in-the-works); [Thai PBS World](https://www.thaipbsworld.com/crime/move-forward-mp-to-propose-controlled-use-of-avs-and-sex-toys)
- Malaysia: sale prohibited under Section 7(1) of the Printing Presses and Publications Act; Penal Code s.292 obscene objects; 1,240 toys seized at Port Klang (Aug 2026); RM1m stash seized in Puchong (Jan 2026); 11,000 toys seized (Jun 2024) — [Malay Mail on PPPA](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/02/29/previously-used-against-watches-and-now-sex-toys-lawyers-explain-the-breadth-of-malaysias-printing-laws/120369); [The Star, Aug 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/14/over-1200-sex-toys-seized-by-customs-dept-at-port-klang); [Malay Mail, Jan 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/01/06/public-tip-off-leads-customs-to-rm1m-sex-toy-stash-in-puchong-warehouse/204479); [FMT, Jun 2024](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2024/06/05/customs-seize-11000-sex-toys-worth-more-than-rm2mil)
- Indonesia: officials have burned seized sex toys (Bali airport; Pontianak customs 2015); legal status rests on vaguely worded pornography law that may cover them — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/wx8j9x/indonesia-burned-hundreds-of-sex-toys-amid-spiking-demand); [Jakarta Post 2015](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2015/12/17/sex-toys-porn-destroyed-pontianak-customs); [The Brag](https://thebrag.com/bali-airport-officials-burn-100000-worth-of-sex-toys-and-other-contraband); one travel source says no specific restriction on personal toys — [Goods Across Borders: Indonesia](https://www.goodsacrossborders.com/traveling/can-i-bring/sex-toys/to/indonesia/print) (conflict)
- Vietnam: customs policy against importing sex toys (held at entry, returned on exit per a 2011 announcement); possession potentially challengeable under Penal Code Art. 155 — [Pretoria News, Jan 2025](https://pretorianews.co.za/saturday-star/news/2025-01-24-when-pleasure-meets-the-law-countries-with-sex-toy-bans); [Goods Across Borders: Vietnam](https://www.goodsacrossborders.com/traveling/can-i-bring/sex-toys/to/vietnam/print) (says status ambiguous; low reliability)
- South Korea: customs official said (2014) there are no specific rules on importing adult toys; 34,000+ adult items on G Market (2014); Korea Customs Service allowed adult-shaped sex doll imports from Dec 2022 while banning child-like dolls — [Korea Times 2014](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/lifestyle/others/20140401/as-colorful-as-candy-store); [WION 2022](https://www.wionews.com/world/south-korea-lifts-ban-on-import-of-adult-sized-sex-dolls-customs-office-come-up-with-revised-guidelines-547086/amp)
- Japan: third-party customs card says personal-use toys may be brought in freely — [Goods Across Borders: Japan](https://www.goodsacrossborders.com/traveling/can-i-bring/sex-toys/to/japan/print) (third-party, not Japan Customs)
- Maldives customs prohibits import of pornographic materials including sex toys (incidental finding) — [Pretoria News](https://pretorianews.co.za/saturday-star/news/2025-01-24-when-pleasure-meets-the-law-countries-with-sex-toy-bans)

### Inferences
- Japan, South Korea: OK to show links (legal domestic market). Candidate routes: Amazon JP, global brands (LELO, Satisfyer); domestic affiliate options (Rakuten, Coupang Partners, A8.net, Korean shops) were not researched successfully.
- Singapore, Philippines: show only neutral items (candles, massage oil, blindfold) or a global brand pending legal check; Shopee/Lazada are not usable for toys.
- Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam (and Maldives, Brunei by analogy, unverified): show no links.
- Shopee/Lazada affiliate programs (via Involve Asia etc.) are effectively not usable for toys anywhere in SEA; even candles/oils would need checking against "sexual wellness" content rules.

### Gaps
- Singapore legal status of sex toy sale/advertising (believed legal for adults, but no source found here); Philippines status (no source found).
- Japan and Korea affiliate programs for this category (Rakuten, Amazon JP adult category rules, Coupang Partners).
- Involve Asia / Admitad directories for any SEA sexual wellness advertiser not checked.
- Brunei not researched (likely restrictive; unverified).

## 4. Countries where sale, import or advertising is illegal or seriously restricted (show no links)

### Takeaway
Show no links in: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Iran, Pakistan, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Maldives, and (pending evidence) Bangladesh and Brunei. The documentation is strongest for Malaysia, Thailand, Bahrain, UAE and Saudi Arabia, and weakest for Oman, Kuwait and Bangladesh. Most sources are shipping guides, travel pieces and news reports, not statutes; local legal advice is needed before relaxing any of these.

- Bahrain: US military transport guidance lists sex toys as prohibited; Bahrain customs x-rays all shipments and destroys confiscated items; a Toronto store received warning letters (2025/2026) after shipments were flagged — [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/toronto/article/stop-sending-butt-plugs-to-bahrain-toronto-sex-store-receives-letters-from-us-department-of-war/)
