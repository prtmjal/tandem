# Sexual wellness affiliate links from a new couples app (India-based founder): practicalities, late 2026

Research method note: web search only (about 17 queries). Direct fetches of awin.com and developers.google.com failed because those hosts are not on this session's allowed network list, so Awin's publisher terms PDF and Google's outbound-links page were only seen through search snippets. Most commission/EPC figures come from aggregator pages (FlexOffers, affi.io, LinkClicky, Grips) that are undated or old. Nothing here is tax or legal advice; items marked "accountant" need a Chartered Accountant (CA) familiar with export of services.

## 1. Which networks and programs accept adult / sexual-wellness publishers, and what do they require of a brand-new site?

### Takeaway
Sexual-wellness brands sit on mainstream networks (Awin, Impact, FlexOffers, Commission Factory, CJ-style networks, Cuelinks for Indian brands), so the networks themselves are reachable; the real gate is each brand's program-level approval. Networks treat "adult / NSFW" as a restricted category, not a banned one. No source found states a hard domain-age or traffic minimum for any network; expect manual review where a thin, brand-new site is the main risk.

### Cited Findings
**Where the brands are (program-level availability, mostly aggregator data, verify on each network):**
- Lovehoney: has moved networks over time; Webgains announced it took over the programme (older press release) — [Webgains](https://www.webgains.com/public/en/lovehoney-revitalises-affiliate-programme-with-a-move-to-webgains); an aggregator now lists a US program on Awin and an AU/NZ program on Commission Factory with a 30-day cookie and 16-18% revenue share — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/lovehoney); Commission Factory has run Lovehoney "advertiser spotlight" and "affiliate day" posts — [Commission Factory](https://www.commissionfactory.com/market-insights/affiliate-marketing/advertiser-spotlight-lovehoney); FlexOffers lists Lovehoney (US, CA, DE, FR) programs with a 30-day cookie — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/lovehoney-affiliate-program/).
- Lelo: FlexOffers lists 12% of sale — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/lelo-affiliate-program/); another page says 5-20% — [affpaying](https://www.affpaying.com/lelo) (conflict; undated); Lelo DE listed on Awin — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/lelo-de).
- We-Vibe: listed on Impact with 1-20% per sale — [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/we-vibe/); North America program on FlexOffers, 30-day cookie — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/we-vibe-north-america-affiliate-program/).
- Others on FlexOffers: Tenga EU (4-16% by SKU/code), B-Vibe (8%), Hot Octopuss DE, Satisfyer IE, Wild Secrets (10%, 5% with coupon) — [FlexOffers Tenga EU](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/tenga-eu-affiliate-program/), [FlexOffers B-Vibe](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/b-vibe-affiliate-program/), [FlexOffers Hot Octopuss](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/hot-octopuss-de-affiliate-program/), [FlexOffers Satisfyer IE](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/satisfyer-ie-affiliate-program/).
- Dipsea (audio erotica app, a natural "content" partner) has an "Opened" program on Impact — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/dipsea).
- India (Cuelinks): Cuelinks lists a campaign for Cake (sexual wellness: lubricants, hygiene) — [Cuelinks Cake](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/cake-affiliate-program); a Sexshopara campaign is listed but paused — [Cuelinks Sexshopara](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/sexshopara-affiliate-program); Cuelinks also lists The Women Company and Giddy Health — [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/the-women-company-affiliate-program).

**Network stance on adult content:**
- Impact: its brand help centre lists "adult content (age-restricted 18+ or NSFW)" among restricted product categories (alongside CBD, vaping, gambling); brands that sell these flag it so Impact adjusts recommended partners. This is a recommendation filter, not a ban — [Impact help](https://help.impact.com/brand/readme/performance-program/recruiting-partners/avoid-recommended-partners-with-promotion-limits). Impact's terms reserve the right to remove content it judges objectionable in its sole discretion — [ConductAtlas summary of Impact terms](https://conductatlas.com/platform/impact/impact-terms-and-conditions/provision/CA-P-067559/prohibition-on-objectionable-content-at-impacts-discretion/).
- Cuelinks: its blog says several niches (gambling, adult, some finance/medical) are disallowed on AdSense-type platforms; it is unclear whether this describes Cuelinks' own rule — [Cuelinks blog](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/best-google-adsense-alternative-bloggers/).
- Awin: new publishers pay a refundable 1 EUR/GBP/USD verification fee (older third-party pages cite $5); Awin says it is refunded with the first payment once the threshold is met, is not refunded automatically, and no invoice is issued; an advertiser invitation code can bypass it (third-party claim) — [Awin success centre](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Why-do-I-have-to-pay-the-sign-up-fee); [Awin wiki FAQ](https://wiki.awin.com/index.php/FAQ_Publisher_Admin).

### Inferences
- Realistic order for a new site: (1) FlexOffers and Impact (brand-by-brand applications, low friction), (2) Awin (1-unit deposit, then apply to Lovehoney US / Lelo etc.), (3) direct in-house programs on brand sites (Shopify apps like Refersion/GoAffPro/UpPromote) for D2C brands, (4) Cuelinks or brand-direct for Indian brands like Cake, mainly if the audience is Indian.
- Approval risk for a brand-new domain is mostly about looking like a real, finished, 18+-gated product with an About page, privacy page, contact address and a clear description of where links will appear ("in-app wellness shelf on the map screen, not during play"). The app already has `/privacy` and an 18+ gate, which helps.
- Describe the traffic honestly as "web app (PWA) + landing page" — app/in-app placements are a distinct promotional method that some programs exclude.

- No source confirms whether ShareASale still exists as a separate platform (it was migrated into Awin; not verified here).

## 2. Getting paid in India: thresholds, methods, currency, tax (GST, income tax, W-8BEN)

### Takeaway
Thresholds are low (Impact $10, Awin £20/€20 with a 50-unit default, Cuelinks ₹500), and bank transfer or Payoneer covers all major networks. Foreign commissions are generally treated as zero-rated export of services under GST (file an LUT), but the intermediary place-of-supply rule creates real ambiguity, so a CA should classify it. US-source withholding is avoided by filing W-8BEN, but which treaty article applies is disputed among sources.

### Cited Findings
**Thresholds and methods**
- Impact: minimum withdrawal $10 USD or local equivalent; choose threshold-based or fixed monthly withdrawals; "Anytime PayPal" option; bank details must be verified before PayPal is available; 48-hour hold after changing bank details; PayPal requires a supported account currency — [Impact help: select how often you get paid](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/select-how-often-you-get-paid-as-a-partner); [Impact help: PayPal](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/withdraw-funds-to-your-paypal-account). No India-specific rule found.
- Awin: publisher sets a threshold; minimum £20 (GBP) / €20 (EUR); default 50 GBP/EUR/USD if not set — [Awin success centre](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/What-are-the-payment-thresholds). India-specific transfer details not found.
- CJ: $50 direct deposit / $100 cheque per Namecheap's table — [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/9994/2241/payment-options-and-minimum-payout-limits-for-affiliates); a 2026 blog says $100 for all methods — [EarnifyHub](https://www.earnifyhub.com/blog/affiliate/cj-affiliate-vs-awin-international) (conflict, unresolved). CJ can pay into a Payoneer USD receiving account — [Payoneer CJ partner page](https://pages.payoneer.com/partner/cj-affiliate/); direct deposit to India has been reported as available — [ShoutMeLoud](https://www.shoutmeloud.com/receive-commission-junction-affiliate-payment-payoneer.html) (older).
- Cuelinks: ₹500 minimum for Indian bank accounts (NEFT/RTGS), ₹10,000 for overseas accounts — [Cuelinks via search summary](https://desk.zoho.com/portal/cuelinks/kb/articles/what-are-the-payment-dates); [UpPromote directory](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/cuelinks/).

**GST**
- Commissions from foreign affiliate programs are commonly treated as zero-rated export of services: no GST charged, but file a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) and issue an export invoice — [Disytax](https://disytax.com/gst-on-foreign-payments/); [Skydo](https://cdn.skydo.com/blog/export-of-services-under-gst).
- Indian programs (e.g., Cuelinks/Indian brands): 18% GST on commission once registered — [Disytax](https://disytax.com/gst-on-foreign-payments/).
- Counterpoint: for intermediary/commission services the place of supply can be the supplier's location in India, making the commission taxable even if the principal is foreign — [Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/treatment-for-commission-agents-working-with-foreign-principals/). Raising an invoice on a foreign client does not by itself make it an export — [Kanakkupillai](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/gst-on-foreign-clients/).
- Registration turnover counts all affiliate commissions, domestic and foreign, together — [Disytax](https://disytax.com/category/gst-for-freelancers-digital-businesses/).

**Income tax**
- Section 44AD presumptive scheme: small businesses up to ₹2 crore turnover; deemed income 8% of turnover (6% for digital receipts per common summaries); opting in forgoes expense deductions — [Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-44ad-5); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/presumptive-taxation-scheme-business-section-44ad-income-tax-act.html). The search summary said the section mentions persons earning "commission or brokerage" income; from background knowledge (not verified in this session), section 44AD(6) lists such persons as EXCLUDED from the scheme, so affiliate commissions may need to be taxed as normal business income (books of account, actual expenses, slab rates). This conflict is unresolved — accountant.

**US forms**
- Without a W-8BEN, a US payer must withhold 30%; the form's claims are valid for three years — [Chron](https://smallbusiness.chron.com/tax-consequences-affiliate-marketing-foreign-countries-82006.html).
- Indian guides suggest claiming India-US treaty Article 7 (business profits, no US permanent establishment) or Article 15 (independent personal services), typically giving 0% withholding on service income; Article 12 royalties are 15% — [Skydo](https://skydo.com/blog/filing-w-8ben-and-w-8ben-e-a-guide-for-indian-freelancers-and-businesses); [Wisemonk](https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/foreign-tds-and-dtaa-getting-credit-for-tax-withheld-by-us-clients). Sources disagree on which article fits affiliate commissions.

### Inferences
- Affiliate commissions for services performed in India are generally not US-source income, so many networks will not withhold at all once a W-8BEN is on file; the treaty claim matters mainly as a belt-and-braces measure. Use W-8BEN-E if paid via a company.
- Practical setup: one bank account that receives foreign remittances with an export purpose code, plus Payoneer as fallback for CJ; keep FIRA/e-BRC records per payout to support GST zero-rating (needs confirming with the bank).
- Currency conversion: bank SWIFT transfers usually carry fees on both ends plus FX markup; Payoneer charges a conversion fee. Exact fees were not found — compare at setup.

### Gaps
- Payoneer/PayPal India fees, FIRA/e-BRC issuance and RBI purpose codes not verified (searches returned nothing primary).
- No ClearTax page surfaced; no authoritative ruling on GST intermediary classification for affiliate commissions.
- No primary source on India GST registration threshold for exporters of services (₹20 lakh commonly cited, not verified here).

## 3. Disclosure, rel="sponsored", cloaking/redirects, deep links, privacy-safe click counting

### Takeaway
Put a short, plain label ("Paid link" / "We earn a commission if you buy") right next to each link, not in a footer; mark links rel="sponsored" (nofollow optional); a first-party /go/ redirect is generally fine on Awin/CJ at network level but some individual programs forbid masking, so check each program's terms and never pre-load or auto-fire a link.

### Cited Findings
- Google: affiliate links should be qualified with rel="sponsored"; affiliate links are acceptable monetization, but failing to qualify them can bring manual actions — [Google Search Central blog, July 2021](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2021/07/link-tagging-and-link-spam-update); [Google docs: qualify outbound links](https://developers.google.cn/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links?hl=en). Applies whether links are made manually or dynamically — [ShoutMeLoud summary](https://www.shoutmeloud.com/google-affiliate-links-seo.html).
- FTC (US audience): 2023 Endorsement Guides define "clear and conspicuous" as "difficult to miss ... and easily understandable"; FTC FAQ commentary says "affiliate link" alone or "commissionable link" is not clear enough, while "paid link" next to the link can work; bottom-of-page disclosures are unlikely to comply; disclosures behind "more" are not conspicuous — [Manatt](https://manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-2); [PartnerCentric](https://partnercentric.com/blog/how-to-properly-disclose-ftc-endorsements/); [Termly](https://termly.io/resources/articles/ftc-affiliate-disclosure).
- India (ASCI): influencer guidelines (in force since 14 June 2021) require upfront labels; final approved labels are Advertisement, Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Employee, Free gift; disclosure must not be buried in hashtags/links and must be visible without "click more"; ASCI codes are self-regulatory — [Khaitan & Co](https://khaitanco.com/thought-leaderships/ASCI-releases-influencer-advertising-guidelines-for-digital-media-Effective-14-June-21-onward); [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/article/companies/if-it-s-an-ad-disclose-it-upfront-asci-guidelines-for-digital-influencers-121022100884_1.html); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/advertising-standards-for-social-media-influencers). Not explicitly about affiliate links (inference that it applies).
- Cloaking: Amazon Associates bars shortening that hides the destination; a secondary review found no network-wide cloaking ban in Awin or CJ public terms, with restrictions set by individual programs — [QualityUnit](https://cdn.qualityunit.com/faq/should-you-cloak-affiliate-links); [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/affiliate-link-cloaking-why-brands-restrict-it-and-what-disclosure-rules-still-apply). Some programs ban cloaked links because they hide traffic sources — [QualityUnit](https://cdn.qualityunit.com/faq/what-does-link-cloaking-do).
- Cookie stuffing (dropping affiliate cookies without a genuine click) is prohibited and leads to reversed commissions and termination — [Involve Asia glossary](https://involve.asia/?p=69981); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookie_stuffing); [Ben Edelman](https://www.benedelman.org/?p=1723).

### Inferences
- Suggested wording for the app: a small label beside each product tile, "Paid link: we may earn a commission. Prices are the same for you.", plus one line on the landing/privacy page. Neutral ink, not hidden behind a tap.
- Implementation fitting the repo's rules: a server route `/go/:slug` that looks up a static map of slug -> network deep link, increments a daily total in `server/stats.ts` (e.g. `affiliate_click`), and returns a 302. Log no IP, room code, user agent or which card/level the player was on (CLAUDE.md rule 10). Send only a static sub-ID per placement (e.g. "map-shelf"), never a per-user ID. Mark the anchor `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` and open in the system browser (`target="_blank"`).
- Do not prefetch/preload `/go/` links (link prefetch or server-side follow-through would set cookies without a click = cookie stuffing risk). Exclude `/go/` from service-worker precache and add `Disallow: /go/` in robots.txt.
- Placement: keep links on the map screen (same place the Couple Pass may be offered), never during a night, no countdown/"deal ends" copy, consistent with CLAUDE.md rules 4 and 7. Deep links: use each network's deep-link generator so the 302 lands on a specific product page.

- No source specific to disclosure inside PWAs or apps.

## 4. Benchmarks: conversion, EPC, AOV, commissions, case studies

### Takeaway
Typical program commissions are 8-20% of sale; retailer AOVs cluster at $50-100 for mainstream shops; category conversion rates are roughly 1.5-5%. Published EPCs are thin and low (B-Vibe $0.03-0.07). A small couples app should plan on low tens to low hundreds of dollars a month until traffic is in the tens of thousands of monthly users.

### Cited Findings
- Commissions: Lovehoney 16-18% (aggregator) — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/lovehoney); Lelo 12% or 5-20% — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/lelo-affiliate-program/), [affpaying](https://www.affpaying.com/lelo); We-Vibe 1-20% — [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/we-vibe/); B-Vibe 8%, Tenga 4-16%, Wild Secrets 10% (5% with coupons) — [FlexOffers B-Vibe](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/b-vibe-affiliate-program/), [FlexOffers Tenga EU](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/tenga-eu-affiliate-program/).
- EPC: B-Vibe on FlexOffers $0.07 (90-day) and $0.03 (30-day) — [FlexOffers B-Vibe](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/b-vibe-affiliate-program/). No other EPC figures found.
- AOV / conversion (Grips estimates, ranges): sexual-wellness category 1.5-2.0% conversion, AOV $0-100 (June 2026) — [Grips category](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/health-beauty/condoms-and-sexual-wellness); PinkCherry AOV $50-75 — [Grips](https://d8q56x4xdbci2.cloudfront.net/insights/retailers/pinkcherry.com); Casual Toys 4.5-5.0% conversion, $75-100 AOV (Sept 2024) — [Grips](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/casualtoys.com); Pleasure You Please Me 1.0-1.5% conversion, $300-325 AOV — [Grips](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/pleasureyoupleasureme.com). Bellesa's program page advertises "$80 AOV & 4% conversion" (merchant claim) — per search summary.
- Adam & Eve: new customers from a machine-learning campaign had 13% higher AOV than existing customers (campaign-specific) — [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/commerce/the-adult-retailer-adam-and-eve-embraces-machine-learning-if-it-means-new-customers/).
- Dipsea and Ferly: no reported sex-toy affiliate partnership found; Dipsea runs its own Impact program — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/dipsea); Ferly is a free couples sex-therapy app with in-app purchases — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/ferly/id1449167768).

### Inferences
Illustrative model (assumptions, not sourced data): 1,000 outbound clicks/month x 2% conversion x $70 AOV x 12% commission = about $168/month, i.e. EPC about $0.17. At 5% click-through from 10,000 monthly active couples (500 clicks) that is roughly $80/month. Halve for attribution loss (section 5) and returns. Bundled "couples" products and higher-AOV brands (Lelo, We-Vibe) raise EPC more than raw traffic does.

- No credible 2025-2026 industry-wide EPC or conversion benchmark for sexual wellness affiliates; Awin/Authority Hacker/Affiliate Summit reports not reached.
- No verified case study of a sex educator, blog or app's affiliate earnings.

## 5. Risks: incentivized/in-app traffic, cookie stuffing, webviews and Safari ITP

### Takeaway
The main risks are (a) programs rejecting or reversing "app" or "incentivized" traffic, (b) cookie-stuffing rules triggered by prefetching, and (c) attribution loss on iPhone Safari and inside social-app webviews, which can cut credited sales meaningfully.

### Cited Findings
- Safari ITP limits script-set cookies to 7 days and some click IDs to 24 hours; ITP 2.2 (2019) cut link-decorated cookies to one day — [Track360](https://track360.io/blog/affiliate-tracking-technology-evolution-timeline-2026); [Awin news](https://www.awin.com/us/news-and-events/industry-news/cookie-periods-shorten-even-further-with-additional-changes-to-safaris-itp-update); [Optimise Media](https://optimisemedia.com/blog/itp2.2-anti-tracking-update/).
- An Econsultancy/Littledata study of 812 stores estimated about 8% attribution loss for Safari mobile (2018-2019 data, old) and noted server-set cookies fare better — [Econsultancy](https://econsultancy.com/what-impact-is-safari-itp-having-on-marketing-attribution/).
- A September 2025 Safari change limits cookies to 7 days for responses from third-party IPs (initially beta) — [Stape](https://stape.io/de/blog/safari-itp-update-limits-cookies-to-7-days-for-responses-from-3rd-party-ips). Status unverified.
- Networks increasingly push server-side (S2S) tracking — [Scaleo](https://www.scaleo.io/blog/safari-itp-is-killing-affiliate-program-attribution/) (vendor claim).
- Cookie stuffing leads to reversal and termination — see section 3 sources.

### Inferences
- Opening links in the system browser (not an in-app webview) and avoiding delays between click and purchase are the main levers a publisher controls; most attribution now depends on the merchant's server-side tracking.
- Avoid anything that could look "incentivized": no unlocking cards, Couple Pass discounts or game progress for clicking or buying. This also matches CLAUDE.md rule 7.
- Because players are often on phones in a private moment, purchase is likely deferred; short Safari cookie windows will hurt more than for desktop-heavy blogs.

### Gaps
- No source directly quantified in-app webview (Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp) attribution loss.
- No primary network terms found on "incentivized" or "app" traffic definitions for Awin/Impact/CJ.
