### Inferences
- If the RankHero figures are Google-derived, "couples games" and "couple game" are mid-volume head terms (~50-75K/mo) with low CPC ($0.20-0.70). Low CPC means little advertiser competition. RankHero's KD scores of 34-38 are not comparable with Ahrefs KD.
- The singular and no-"s" form ("couple game") reportedly matches the plural in volume. "Couple game" phrasing is typical of Indian and South Asian English usage, which matters for an India focus. This is unverified because no country split was available.
- Question-list keywords ("deep/spicy questions for couples", "truth or dare for couples") are dominated by content publishers. An exact-match domain would give little ranking advantage there; content depth and backlinks matter more.
- "Date night ideas" (~40K US) is a larger but broader, less game-specific term.

## Q2. Google Trends: 5-year interest, Valentine's seasonality, top countries (is India high?)

### Takeaway
No Google Trends data could be retrieved (trends.google.com not reachable; no published Trends breakdown found for these terms). The only seasonality evidence is indirect: Valentine's-related US searches roughly double from January to February, and "couples games" ranged from 49.5K to 74K/month over Feb 2025 to Jan 2026 (RankHero).

### Cited Findings
- US Valentine's-related searches rose from 17.2 million in January to 37.9 million in February, driven by gift, date-idea and card planning — [Semrush News](https://www.semrush.com/news/380949-valentines-day-a-data-driven-love-story-of-trends-searches-and-online-dating/) (year not stated in the snippet).
- "couples games" monthly volume ranged from 49,500 to 74,000 between Feb 2025 and Jan 2026 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/couples-games). The snippet did not say which months peaked.
- An Accio business article reports a December peak for English "board games for couples" — [Accio, trend of games for couples 2025](https://www.accio.com/business/trend-of-games-for-couples). Its data source is unclear: low reliability.

### Inferences
- A February (Valentine's) bump and a December (gift season) bump are plausible for game and date-night terms, but neither was confirmed with Trends data for the exact keywords.

### Gaps
- 5-year relative interest, top countries and India's rank for "couples games" / "couple game" / "questions for couples" are **unknown**. These need a manual Google Trends check (Past 5 years, Worldwide, "Interest by region") for: couple game, couples games, questions for couples, truth or dare for couples, date night ideas.
