## Registration and renewal prices by TLD (.com, .app, .co, .in, .game)

### Takeaway
.com costs about $10-11 a year at at-cost registrars, .app about $14-15, and .co about $30. .game is far more expensive, at about $300-340 a year at Porkbun (stale figure), and that cost recurs every year at renewal.

### Cited Findings
- .com: about $11/year at Porkbun, with registration and renewal at the same price. Namecheap renews .com at roughly $15-18. — [domainoffer.net](https://domainoffer.net/blog/namecheap-vs-godaddy-vs-spaceship-vs-porkbun); [dev.to](https://dev.to/paullovelace/4-domain-registrars-ranked-by-renewal-price-october-2026-4eo0)
- .app: Cloudflare renews at $14.20 and Porkbun at $14.93. Dynadot offers a first year from $8.00 that renews at $14.50. Over 10 years, .app costs about $136 at Cloudflare. — [Porkbun .app calculator](https://porkbun.com/about/domain-cost-calculator/app.txt); [costgoat](https://costgoat.com/compare/domains/app)
- .co: Porkbun renews at $31.20 and Cloudflare at $30. — [Porkbun .co calculator](https://porkbun.com/about/domain-cost-calculator/co.txt)
- .in: no reliable 2026 plain-.in renewal figure was found. Porkbun's .co.in is listed at a $4.80 first year and $5.80 renewal. — [domainoffer.net .co.in Porkbun](https://domainoffer.net/tld/co.in/porkbun)
- .game: Porkbun has listed about $340/year regular, with a first-year sale near $309. A separate page showed about $333. These pages appear to be old. — [Porkbun domains](https://porkbun.com/products/domains); [Porkbun ACH pricing](https://porkbun.com/products/domains_ach)
- .app is HSTS-preloaded and requires HTTPS. This is general knowledge about the TLD, not a cited source.

### Inferences
- tandem.game would cost about $3,000+ over 10 years in renewals alone, roughly the same as buying couplesgames.com outright.
- Premium-tier names in .app and .game can carry higher prices than these standard rates. Short dictionary names like tandem.game are especially likely to be premium.

### Gaps
- The .game pricing is stale. Namecheap and Cloudflare .game prices were not found. Plain .in pricing was not found.

## "Tandem" trademark and name-conflict risk

### Takeaway
"Tandem" is crowded with trademarks in the classes most relevant to apps and games. Tandem Diabetes Care's TANDEM word mark includes class 41, which covers entertainment and education. The Tandem language-exchange app holds TANDEM in classes 9, 38 and 42, which cover software and online services. playtandem.com is a children's app. A distinctive compound such as "Tandem After Dark" lowers the risk but does not remove it, and the overlap with a kids' brand is a reputational concern for an adult game.

### Cited Findings
- Tandem Diabetes Care holds a TANDEM word mark, registered in 2023, covering classes 9, 10, 41 and 44, plus TANDEM SOURCE and TANDEM MOBI. — [Justia: TANDEM SOURCE](https://trademark.justia.com/903/98/tandem-90398407.html); [markinton: tandem 97566932](https://markinton.com/trademark/tandem-97566932); [Tandem Diabetes IP page](https://tandemdiabetes.com/legal/patents)
- Tripod Technology GmbH (Hannover, later Berlin), which runs the Tandem language-exchange app with 10M+ Play Store downloads, holds TANDEM US Reg. #5592755 (Serial 86620583) in classes 9, 38 and 42, registered 30 Oct 2018. It also holds Reg. #4328173 in class 45. These details come from a secondary aggregator; verify at the USPTO. — [markinton: tandem 86620583](https://markinton.com/trademark/tandem-86620583); [markinton: tandem 85724557](https://markinton.com/trademark/tandem-85724557); [Google Play: Tandem](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.tandem); [Wikipedia: Tandem (app)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandem_(app))
- Early Ideas Ltd runs "Tandem" at playtandem.com, a children's story app for ages 2-8. — [playtandem.com](https://www.playtandem.com/)
- *Tandem: A Tale of Shadows* is a 2021 puzzle video game, so the name is also used in games. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandem:_A_Tale_of_Shadows)
- A Unified Patent Court record lists Tandem Diabetes Care as a party in a 2025 appeal. The subject matter is not shown and the record is not related to naming. — [UPC](https://www.unified-patent-court.org/fr/node/126177)

### Inferences
- tandemgame.app, tandem.game and playtandem-style names sit closest to existing class 9 and 41 TANDEM marks. tandemafterdark.com is the most distinguishable of the tandem-named options. A trademark clearance search (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO) is advisable before committing.
- Sharing a name with a children's app (playtandem.com) creates search confusion and brand-safety risk for an 18+ product.

### Gaps
- No sourced information was found on Tandem Bank's UK trademarks or on any Tandem trademark disputes. A USPTO/EUIPO search was not possible from this session.
