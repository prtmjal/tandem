# Search competition for couples-game keywords (2025-2026)

**How this was gathered (read first).** Researched 2026-11-05.
- **Tool data: none.** The Ahrefs connector rejected every call, including `serp-overview` for "couples games", "questions for couples" and "truth or dare for couples" (US) and "couple games" (IN), and the free `public-domain-rating-free` endpoint. Each failed with "Insufficient plan". So these notes contain **no DR/DA numbers, no keyword difficulty and no Google volumes from Ahrefs or Semrush**.
- **Live Google results: not available.** Fetching google.com, rankhero.com, couplesgames.com and questionsforcouples.com failed because those hosts are outside this session's allowed network.
- **What the notes rest on:** results from the agent's WebSearch tool. It is US-only and **is not a Google SERP**. Its ranking order and features (People Also Ask, snippets, videos, app packs) differ from Google's, and it returns a lot of spam (hacked subdomains on .edu, .gov and corporate hosts). Treat "appears in results" below as a sign of who competes, not as a Google position.
- Authority labels such as "high authority" are **my inference** from brand size. They are not measured DR.

## 1. Which sites compete in each keyword cluster

### Takeaway
Every cluster is led by the same three kinds of site:
- large relationship and wedding publishers: Marriage.com, The Knot, BetterHelp, Bustle, Calm, Psych Central, MomJunction, StyleCraze, Bonobology
- couples-app companies running content blogs: Paired above all, then Candle, Amora, Privé, Pairplay, Flamme, Connected Couples
- for the spicy and truth-or-dare cluster, a crowd of iOS apps and free generator tools

Paired shows up in almost every cluster, which makes it the main content competitor for an app like Tandem.

- The spicy, truth-or-dare and couple-quiz clusters look the most winnable for a new site. They are fragmented across small app blogs (privegame, tryamora, pairplaycouples, lovecouplez, quiz-couple) and generator tools. - Many app companies (Paired, Candle, Amora, Privé, Pairplay, Flamme, Connected Couples) rank through question-list blog posts, not homepages. Ranking appears to depend on content pages, whatever the domain name.

## 2. Exact-match and partial-match domains that rank

### Takeaway
No exact-match domain for the head terms (couplesgames.com, questionsforcouples.com, couplequiz.com) appeared in any result set. The keyword-rich domains that did appear are partial matches, usually with a modifier or another TLD, and they rank for long-tail spicy or quiz phrases next to big brands. Big brands still dominate the head terms.

### Cited Findings
- **quiz-couple.com** ranks for "spicy questions for couples" with a 30-question "Spicy Couple Quiz". — [quiz-couple.com](https://quiz-couple.com/en/spicy-couple-quiz/)
- **lovecouplez.com** ("190+ Spicy Intimate Questions for Couples (2025)"). — [lovecouplez.com](https://lovecouplez.com/spicy-intimate-questions-for-couples-in-love/)
- **pairplaycouples.app**. — [pairplaycouples.app](https://pairplaycouples.app/blogs/25-spicy-questions-to-ask-your-partner)
- **truthordareai.com** ranks for "truth or dare generator" and sells a paid plan. — [truthordareai.com](https://www.truthordareai.com/)
- **getcouplezone.com** shows up for both "couple games online" and "long distance relationship games". — [getcouplezone](https://getcouplezone.com/blog/long-distance-relationship-games.html)
- **connectedcouples.app** ranks with a page at /couples-games-online. — [Connected Couples](https://www.connectedcouples.app/couples-games-online)
- **coupletraveltheworld.com** ranks for "couple quiz", which shows a "couple" domain from a different niche competing. — [link](https://coupletraveltheworld.com/couples-quiz/)
- **lastingthedistance.com** is a niche domain for the LDR cluster. — [link](https://lastingthedistance.com/?p=8389)
- Searching for "questionsforcouples", "couplesgames.com" and "truthordaregame" brought back no pages from those domains. — [search result set incl. Marriage.com, Paired, StyleCraze](https://www.marriage.com/advice/relationship/truth-or-dare-questions-for-couples/)
- Couples Game Night, a browser-based two-phone 18+ game, is the closest direct rival found. Its domain was not shown in the result. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/couples-game-night/about/)

### Inferences
- Partial-match domains such as lovecouplez, quiz-couple and truthordareai seem to win long-tail spicy and quiz terms, probably through topical focus and exact-phrase titles more than the domain name itself. Google stopped giving exact-match domains a special boost with its 2012 EMD update (background knowledge, not checked here).
- Paired, Candle, Amora and Privé rank without any keyword in the domain. That suggests a brandable name plus strong content pages is a workable route for Tandem.

### Gaps
- Could not check whether couplesgames.com, questionsforcouples.com, couplequiz.com or truthordare.com are registered, parked, for sale or active. The fetches were blocked by session network settings, so the hosts must be allowed before this can be checked.
- No backlink or authority data for any partial-match domain.

### Inferences
- Google probably shows People Also Ask and listicle-driven featured snippets for the question-list queries, given that the ranking pages are numbered lists. This is **unverified**, and the report should not state it as fact.

### Gaps
- PAA, featured snippets, video and TikTok carousels, app packs, AI Overviews and "Things to know" panels could not be checked. Doing so needs live Google access or a working Ahrefs `serp-overview`, which requires a higher plan.

## 4. India: what ranks for "couple games" and "couple questions"

### Takeaway
No India-localised results could be pulled, because the search tool is US-only and Ahrefs IN SERP was refused. Indirect evidence points to Bonobology as the main Indian relationship publisher with a deep set of couple-game pages. Indian queries that mix "couple" with "games" also pull heavily toward wedding-game content.

### Cited Findings
- Bonobology has many matching pages: "25 Games To Play With Your Girlfriend", "Romantic Games For Couples", "Conversation Games For Couples", "Texting Games", "Party Games For Couples", "Board Games For Couples", "Long-Distance Relationship Games". — [Bonobology girlfriend games](https://www.bonobology.com/games-to-play-with-your-girlfriend/); [romantic games](https://www.bonobology.com/romantic-games-for-couples/); [conversation games](https://www.bonobology.com/conversation-games-for-couples/); [texting games](https://www.bonobology.com/texting-games-to-play/)
- BollywoodShaadis publishes "30 Couple Games To Play With Your Partner" on Medium. — [Medium/BollywoodShaadis](https://medium.com/bollywoodshaadis-com/30-couple-games-to-play-with-your-partner-to-have-fun-while-spending-quality-time-with-each-other-23f082ef8525)
- The query "couple games ... India ... questions" returned mostly wedding-game content: WeddingWire India (several "games" pages), Bookeventz, ShaadiDukaan, Wedding Bazaar, VenueLook ("Indian Wedding Games List ... 2026"). — [WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/indian-couple-wedding-games--c5565); [ShaadiDukaan](https://www.shaadidukaan.com/blog/super-fun-indian-wedding-games-ideas.html/amp); [VenueLook](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/indian-wedding-games-list-fun-activities-for-mehndi-sangeet-haldi-and-reception-2026)
- Some couples apps list themselves on the Indian App Store, for example "Games for Couples - Spicy". — [App Store IN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/games-for-couples-spicy/id933524873)
- Hindi-language queries such as पति पत्नी सवाल जवाब ("husband wife question-answer game") were not tested.

### Inferences
- In India, "couple games" carries mixed intent: wedding and sangeet games versus games for two partners. An Indian-facing domain or landing page may need wording that rules out the wedding sense, such as "for two", "date night" or "18+".
- Global players (Paired, Marriage.com, The Knot) probably also rank in India, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- No google.co.in top 10 for "couple games" or "couple questions". This needs Ahrefs `serp-overview` with country=IN (plan upgrade) or a manual check from an Indian IP.
- No Indian search volumes, and no Hindi or Hinglish keyword data.
