How privacy works

A plain-language summary of what the game keeps today. This is not yet a full legal privacy policy; one will be published before launch.

On your phone

Your nickname, colour, unlocked levels, cards you've already seen, cards you kept, and your own private “curious” taps are saved in your phone's browser storage. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Your progress lives on your two phones, not on our server. Each game merges both phones' copies, so it's only lost if both phones lose it. To keep it safe, add the game to your home screen (on iPhone this stops Safari clearing it after a quiet spell), or make a backup code in Settings. A backup code holds levels and cards, never your private “curious” taps. It's made on your phone and never sent to us; anyone with the code can restore it, so keep it private.

If either of you has the Couple Pass, its pass code is saved on both phones during a game, so the purchase isn't lost with one phone.

On our server, only during a game

While a game is open, the server holds both nicknames, the room code, and tonight's choices in memory so the two phones can stay in sync. Private choices (how far tonight, yes / maybe / not tonight, match-list answers, “who’s more likely” picks and “curious” taps) are used only to work out the shared result. They are never sent to your partner's phone.

A game closes after 30 minutes with nobody connected, or after 8 hours. When it closes, the server forgets it. Nothing about the game is written to a database.

To stop abuse, the server briefly counts how often a network starts or joins games. Those counts are kept in memory and cleared after an hour.

If you buy the Couple Pass

Payment happens on Stripe's checkout page. Stripe handles your card details and receipt email; we never see your card number. Afterwards your phone keeps a pass code, a signed receipt that contains only the product and Stripe's order reference. We don't keep a list of buyers. On your bank statement the purchase shows as a short, discreet line ending in “GAME”.

Shop suggestions (affiliate links)

After a night and on the map, the game may suggest things like a blindfold or massage oil, with links to shops. Once a shop's link pays us a commission, it's marked “Ad · Paid links” and we earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Until then the links are plain, marked “Gift ideas”, and earn us nothing. They never appear during a night.

Suggestions after a night are based only on cards you both saw, never on a card that was quietly swapped away. Your country is guessed on your phone from its time zone so the right shops appear; it's never sent to us. When you tap a link, our server counts one tap in a daily total and sends you to the shop with nothing about you or your game attached. The shop and its affiliate network may set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies. You can turn suggestions off in Settings.

If you join the email list

The optional “Tell me about new decks” sign-up sends only your email address to Klaviyo, the email service we use. It is kept separately from the game: it's never linked to your games, progress, partner or purchases. You'll get a confirmation email first, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Answers and messages

During a night you can send each other messages, or seal an answer so it opens only when you've both answered. Messages pass through our server's memory so both phones can show them, and are deleted when the night ends or the game closes. They are never written to disk, a database or a log. A sealed answer is never sent to your partner's phone until you've both answered; if the card moves on first, it's discarded unopened.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. The game itself never asks for an email or phone number.
  • No ad trackers, and never an ad during your night.
  • No analytics trackers. The server only keeps daily totals, such as how many games started or nights finished, with no cookies, no IDs, no IP addresses and nothing about what you chose. Totals are deleted after 120 days.
  • Fonts are served from our own server, not a third party.

What your partner can work out

Some things can be inferred even though they're never shown. If tonight plays at a lower level than you picked, you can tell your partner picked lower. If you tap “curious” and a level doesn't open, your partner hasn't tapped it yet.

For two adults, 18+.
