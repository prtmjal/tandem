How it works

  1. Start a game. One of you taps the button. That's it.
  2. Send the link. Your partner opens it on their phone. No app, no sign-up.
  3. Choose together. You each pick how far tonight goes, privately. You play the lower pick.
  • Nothing unlocks until you both say yes.
  • Pass anything. No explaining.
  • Your picks stay private. They only see what you both chose.

Privacy, in plain words

  • No account needed to play.
  • Your private picks never reach your partner's phone.
  • Your progress stays on your own phone.
  • The server forgets your game when the night ends.
  • No ads and no ad trackers.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while we're in early access.

Do we both need to download anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. One of you starts a game and sends the link; the other just opens it.

What's in the later levels?

Flirtier and more personal, never graphic. Each level opens only when you both privately say you're curious.

Can we stop anytime?

Yes. Pass any card, pause, or end the night with one tap. Nobody has to explain.

Who is it for?

Couples of any gender or orientation. You both need to be 18 or older.

Does it work long-distance?

It works anywhere you both have a connection, so you can play over a video call. It's designed for nights in the same room.

Which languages?

English for now.
