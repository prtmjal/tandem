How it works

  1. Start a game. One of you taps the button. That's it.
  2. Send the link. Your partner opens it on their phone. No app, no sign-up.
  3. Choose together. You each pick how far tonight goes, privately. You play the lower pick.
  • Nothing unlocks until you both say yes.
  • Pass anything. No explaining.
  • Your picks stay private. They only see what you both chose.

Privacy, in plain words

  • No account needed to play.
  • Your private picks never reach your partner's phone.
  • Your progress stays on your own phone.
  • The server forgets your game when the night ends.
  • No ads and no ad trackers.

Why this exists

We kept asking each other the same questions. Then came the silence.

My partner and I would sit down together, start with “how was your day”, and run out of words before the night had even begun. It was never a lack of love. We had simply run out of new ways in.

So I built the game I wished we'd had. When you're just starting out, it helps you discover each other: the stories, the quirks, the things that make you blush. When you've been together for years, it brings back the slow-burn feeling of being wanted, one question, one look, one dare at a time.

You set the pace together. Nothing unlocks until you both want it, and nobody ever has to explain a pass.

Here's to the nights that start with a question and end somewhere closer.

— P & J, the couple behind Tandem

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while we're in early access.

Do we both need to download anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. One of you starts a game and sends the link; the other just opens it.

What's in the later levels?

Flirtier and more personal, never graphic. Each level opens only when you both privately say you're curious.

Can we stop anytime?

Yes. Pass any card, pause, or end the night with one tap. Nobody has to explain.

Who is it for?

Couples of any gender or orientation. You both need to be 18 or older.

Does it work long-distance?

Yes. Start a video call, then pick “Apart, on a call” when you set up the night. The game skips hands-on cards and keeps the ones that work over video.

Which languages?

English for now.
